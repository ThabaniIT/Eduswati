About EduSwati

Empowering Eswatini's students with quality digital education.

Our Mission

EduSwati was created with a clear mission: to make high-quality education accessible to every student in Eswatini, regardless of their location or socioeconomic background.

We believe that digital technology can bridge educational gaps and provide equal learning opportunities for all students. By offering curriculum-aligned resources that are accessible on mobile devices, we aim to support academic success across the country.

Our platform is designed to complement traditional classroom learning, providing students with additional resources, practice materials, and revision tools to help them excel in their studies.
